Problems

Problem 1 [5 marks]

Evaluate $\int_0^1 x^2\,dx$ from first principles or by antiderivative, and interpret the result as an area.

  1. Compute the antiderivative of $x^2$.
  2. Hence evaluate the definite integral.

Problem 2 [7 marks]

Solve the quadratic equation $$x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$$ and verify each root by substitution.

Problem 3 [8 marks]

A block of mass $m$ slides from rest down a frictionless incline of height $h$. Using conservation of energy,

$$mgh = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2,$$
  1. Find the speed $v$ at the bottom.
  2. What happens to $v$ if the mass is doubled? Justify.

Problem 4 [5 marks]

Prove by induction that for all integers $n \ge 1$, $$\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.$$

Solutions

Solution 1. $\int x^2 dx = x^3/3 + C$, so $\int_0^1 x^2 dx = 1/3$.

Solution 2. $(x-2)(x-3)=0$, hence $x = 2$ or $x = 3$.

Solution 3. $v = \sqrt{2gh}$, independent of $m$; doubling $m$ leaves $v$ unchanged.

Solution 4. Base case $n=1$ holds; assuming the formula for $n$, adding $n+1$ gives $(n+1)(n+2)/2$. ∎
